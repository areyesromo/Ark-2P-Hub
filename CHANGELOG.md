# What's new

What changed in each desktop app release, in plain words. The app shows the new lines on the TV
after it updates (the What's new card), and each GitHub release uses its version's lines as its
notes. Newest first; one `## x.y.z` heading per release and one `- ` line per change.

## 0.1.27

- 🤖 The computer now plays Crazy Eights, Liar's Dice, Anagram Duel and Word Race, at Easy, Normal
  or Hard. These need just your phone against it. It only knows its own cards and dice, plays the
  odds, and takes a person's time to find words.

## 0.1.26

- 🤖 The computer now plays Falling Blocks Battle, Bomb Duel, Kart Race and Kart Race 3D (races and
  balloon battles) and Tower Defence Duel, at Easy, Normal or Hard. In Kart Race it readies up on
  the menus once you have.
- Kart Race: if a player's phone drops out on the setup or standings screen, the other no longer
  waits for them to press Ready (they count as ready, as was always meant).

## 0.1.25

- 🤖 The computer now plays Volley Blobs, Brick Breaker Duel, Rally Tennis, Sumo, Asteroid Duel and
  Mini Golf, at Easy, Normal or Hard.
- 🤖 The computer reacts like a person in the action games: it answers what it sees a human
  reaction time later (a quick player's on Hard, never faster), times what it sees coming with a
  person's error, and has a wobble in its aim and the odd misjudgement.
- With the computer seated, the games it's learning next say ⏳ Coming soon: Falling Blocks Battle,
  Bomb Duel, Kart Race, Tower Defence Duel, the card and word games, the party games, and a
  teammate in the co-op games.

## 0.1.24

- 🤖 The computer now plays the action games too: Pong, Air Hockey, Snake Battle, Light Cycles
  and Tank Duel, and Word Hunt (it searches the grid like you do), at Easy, Normal or Hard.
- Air Hockey: a mallet can no longer push the puck through a wall in a corner, and a puck resting
  in a corner (where no mallet can get behind it) drifts back out after a moment.

## 0.1.23

- 🤖 The computer now plays Connect Four, Checkers, Dots and Boxes, Mancala, Rock Paper Scissors,
  Memory Match, Dice Party, Quiz Battle, Reaction Duel and Trivia Quest (as your teammate), at
  Easy, Normal or Hard.
- With the computer seated, the games it can't play stay in the list, greyed out; the ones coming
  next (Pong, Air Hockey, Snake Battle, Light Cycles, Tank Duel, Word Hunt) say ⏳ Coming soon.

## 0.1.22

- 🤖 Play the computer: with a seat free, pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the TV or your phone (or LT
  on a controller). It plays Battleship, Classic and Ultimate (it picks a commander and uses its
  skills and special), only sees what its own phone would, and its games don't count in the
  leaderboard. Someone joining from the lobby takes its seat.

## 0.1.21

- Ultimate Battleship: every move now plays out on the TV and phones (explosions along the line,
  a torpedo's wake, sonar sweeps, ships going down, frost for Deep Freeze).
- Ultimate Battleship: two new commanders, 🌪️ Admiral Tempest (a cross and a U, and Typhoon) and
  🦑 Captain Kraken (a hidden cross-shaped Leviathan, and Deep Strike down a whole column).
- Ultimate Battleship: two new seas. In the **Fog Bank**, shots into the fog (and what they earn)
  show only on your next turn, and torpedoes lose their way in it. The **Volcano** erupts after
  8 turns and hits every ship square next to the lava; if it sinks both fleets, it's a draw.

## 0.1.20

- **⚡ Ultimate Battleship** (pick it in Battleship's setup): five commanders, each with their own
  fleet (some of it L, T and Z shaped) and a special, ship skills paid with energy (air strikes,
  sonar, torpedoes, shields and more), and four seas with islands, reefs and ice.

## 0.1.19

- Memory Match: a **Race** mode for two phones. The same cards on each phone, you both flip at
  once on your own board, and the first to clear every pair wins.
- Word Race: a **Race** mode. The first to 20, 35 or 50 points takes the round, and words you
  both found count for both.

## 0.1.18

- A **Timer** choice in setup for the games whose clock only keeps things moving: **Timed**
  (the usual times, shown on the button) or **No timer** (a turn or answer waits for you).
  Mind Meld and Trivia Quest now start on No timer (pick Timed for the old clock; in Trivia
  Quest with no timer every right answer hits as hard as a middling-fast one). Fake Out, Crazy
  Eights, Checkers, Mancala, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes, Draw Duel, Battleship and Memory
  Match start timed.

## 0.1.17

- **📱 No TV needed** on five action games: Pong's whole court on your phone (your paddle at
  the bottom), Brick Breaker Duel's wall and balls, Snake Battle's and Light Cycles' arena above
  the pad, and in Tower Defence Duel the creeps on your road plus a small map of your rival's.
- Draw Duel shows your rival's drawing on your phone at the reveal, and Word Race lists their
  words under yours at the end of a round.

## 0.1.16

- Word Hunt: a **Race** mode for two phones. Your own copy of the grid on your phone, your finds
  kept to yourself, and the first to find every word takes it.
- **📱 No TV needed** on 22 more games: the board, card, word and quiz games, where your phone
  shows everything. Fake Out and Liar's Dice now show their reveals on the phones too, and
  the time left for your turn or answer shows in your phone's header.

## 0.1.15

- New co-op game: **Dungeon Duo**, in 3D. A Fighter and a Healer fight down three floors
  together: keys and doors, spikes, a gate one of you holds open, skeletons, and the Stone Warden
  at the bottom. Revive each other when one of you goes down.
- The 3D games (Marble Maze, the 3D minigames and Party Board) are sharper, with soft shadows.

## 0.1.14

- New game: **Kart Race 3D**. Kart Race on a split screen, with a camera behind each kart, so you
  race down the road instead of watching from above. Same racers, tracks, items and battles.
- Going back to the lobby while a game is still loading no longer leaves the TV stuck on it.
- Dice Party: a **📱 Handheld** screen. Your phone shows the whole scorecard, with the dice and
  Roll at the bottom: play it without the TV.
- New labels on the games: **📱 No TV needed** and **🤸 Motion** (tilt or move your phone to
  play).

## 0.1.13

- New game: **Tower Defence Duel**. Build towers to stop the creeps on your road, and spend
  gold to send creeps down your rival's. Run out of lives and you lose.

## 0.1.12

- New card game: **Crazy Eights**, your hand secret on your phone. Match the suit or rank, eights
  are wild, 2s make them draw. First to empty their hand wins. Needs two phones.
- New co-op game: **Kitchen Rush**. Chop, cook, plate up and serve the orders before they run
  out, together. Three kitchens, one split down the middle so you pass things across.

## 0.1.11

- New co-op game: **Trivia Quest**. Answer quiz questions together to beat three monsters (a Goo
  Blob, a Stone Golem and the Dragon) before your shared hearts run out. Both right is a team
  strike; each of you has one 50/50. Up to 9 ⭐.
- New game: **Fake Out**. Write a fake answer to a strange-but-true fact, then spot the real
  one among the fakes. Find the truth for 1000, fool your rival for 500. Needs two phones.

## 0.1.10

- Party Board: two new maps. **🌉 Sky Bridges**: a drawbridge across the middle goes up and
  down every two rounds (up, you go the long way round). **🛒 Market Square**: two shops, and
  prices change every round, from half price to double; the star's price moves too.
- Kart Race: **🎈 Battle** mode. Three balloons each in a wide arena (Donut, Peanut or Plaza);
  every spin-out pops one. Pop all of theirs, or have more left when the 2 minutes are up.

## 0.1.9

- New game: **Volley Blobs**: beach volleyball for two bouncy blobs. Slide, jump and bounce the
  ball over the net; up to 3 touches a side.
- New game: **Brick Breaker Duel**: race to break your wall first, side by side. Power bricks
  shrink your rival's paddle, give you an extra ball or add a row to their wall.
- New game: **Asteroid Duel**: two ships, drifting rocks and edges that wrap round. Turn, thrust
  and shoot; three lives a round.
- New game: **Rally Tennis**: tennis from above. Your player runs to the ball; you time the swing
  and aim. Spot-on timing hits hardest; aim wide and mistime it, and it's out.
- New game: **Falling Blocks Battle**: side-by-side falling blocks with the same pieces for both.
  Clear lines to send junk rows to your rival; fill your well and you lose the round.

## 0.1.8

- **What's new**: after an update, the TV shows what changed. Tap the version number (bottom
  right) to see it again.
- New Party Minigames: **Hurdle Dash** (jump the hurdles in time), **Laser Dodge** (jump the low
  beams, duck the high ones) and **Pop the Most** (pop the balloons in your colour).
- New Party Minigames: **Safe Cracker** (turn the dial until it clicks), **Reflex Grid** (tap the
  lights before they fade) and **Mirror Me** (remember a shape and draw it from memory).
- New Party Minigames: **Cannon Lob** (lob shots over the wall at your rival's tower, with the
  wind) and **Stack Attack** (catch the falling crates and keep your stack from toppling).
- New 3D Party Minigames: **Bumper Cars** (ram your rival in the side or from behind; head-on
  doesn't count) and **Paint Grab** (paint the most floor tiles your colour).

## 0.1.7

- New game: **Anagram Duel**: the same five letters for both of you; the first to make a word
  takes the round.
- New game: **Word Hunt**: a word search race; each word goes to whoever marks it first.

## 0.1.6

- Closing the app is quick again, from Steam's Exit Game and from Quit.

## 0.1.5

- Started from Steam (Game Mode on the Deck), the app no longer opens to a black screen.

## 0.1.4

- The app keeps a log file, to help track down problems.

## 0.1.3

- A step-by-step Steam Deck guide.

## 0.1.2

- The app has its own icon, and each release comes with Steam library pictures.
- The app updates itself: an **Update** banner appears in the lobby when a new version is out.

## 0.1.1

- **Quit** button, and the full screen button works in the app.
- Long setups (Party Board's) fit on the TV.

## 0.1.0

- The first desktop app: an AppImage for the Steam Deck and Linux, and an installer for Windows.
