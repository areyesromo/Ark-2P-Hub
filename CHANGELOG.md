# What's new

What changed in each desktop app release, in plain words. The app shows the new lines on the TV
after it updates (the What's new card), and each GitHub release uses its version's lines as its
notes. Newest first; one `## x.y.z` heading per release and one `- ` line per change.

## 0.1.53

- 📶 Lighter on the Wi-Fi: the TV only receives a race's track, ? boxes and coins when they change
  (Kart Race 3D sends about a third as much to the TV), and each racer's phone gets only its own
  kart's dashboard.
- 🧱 Falling Blocks Battle: shaded blocks, a faint grid in each well, a landing outline in the
  piece's own colour, and the next piece in a box of its own.
- 📳 Pong buzzes when your paddle sends the ball back and on every point; Marble Maze buzzes when
  the marble falls and when it reaches the goal.

## 0.1.52

- 🏎️ Kart Race and Kart Race 3D start like a proper kart game: **choose your racer** (both of you on
  one screen, your pick big on your side with their kart and stats, all twelve along the bottom),
  confirm, then **choose your tyres** (the stats show what they change), confirm, then the **track
  and settings**. On a phone or a controller (D-pad to choose, **A** to confirm, **B** to go back).

## 0.1.51

- 🎬 The six games with their own setup menu (Kart Race, Kart Race 3D, Bomb Duel, Word Race, Mini
  Golf and Memory Match) show their preview beside it too, so every game has one.
- 🧹 The How to play and About buttons are gone from the setup screen: the preview's dialog box tells
  you all of it.
- 📜 The game list's fade no longer covers the first and last games: there's room at the ends, and
  the fade goes once you reach the top or the bottom.

## 0.1.50

- 🎬 Every game's setup screen now shows the game before you play: a picture of it, and a dialog box
  that tells you about it a box at a time (what it is, the rules, the controller buttons, the
  settings). It turns every 10 seconds; **RB** / **RT** turns it straight away, **LB** / **LT** goes
  back, or tap it. The settings are right beside it, on the phones too.

## 0.1.49

- 🎮 The controller reaches the top bar: push up past the first row of games to get to ⚙ Settings,
  🏆 the leaderboard, sound, music and the rest (**A** presses, **↓** comes back). No touching needed.
- 📱 No more scrollbars: it looks like an app, not a web page. Lists still scroll by controller, touch,
  wheel or trackpad, and the game list fades at the edge when there's more to see. The top bar's
  buttons are bigger for a finger on the Steam Deck's screen.

## 0.1.48

- 🃏 Game cards in the lobby have a short line now instead of a long description that spilled over.
  The full description and the rules are on each game's setup screen: **📖 How to play** (the rules,
  the controller buttons and the settings) and **ℹ️ About** (with a controller: **X** and **Y**).
- 🎮 A controller works on the Settings page, the leaderboard and trophies (from the TV's ⚙ and 🏆):
  the D-pad moves between the choices, **A** picks, **B** goes back. No more touchpad scrolling.

## 0.1.47

- 📛 Long names fit their seat: in every layout and theme a name shrinks to fit (up to 16 letters), so
  "Controller 1" no longer pushes Player 2 aside.
- 🤖 With the computer in a seat, the lobby makes room for the games as it does for two people (the
  room code stays on screen: join with it to take the computer's seat).
- 🤖 Reaction Duel, Quiz Battle, Trivia Quest and Tower Stack are for two people now: the computer was
  either unbeatable or no fun there.

## 0.1.46

- 🧩 New: pick a **layout** as well as a theme (Settings, or RT on Player 1's controller). **Versus** puts
  the two of you face to face across the screen with names as big as a scoreboard's, and the games
  as a running order; **Shelf** stands the games on a shelf like boxes, spine out, with the one
  you're on pulled out. Any theme goes with any layout; **Classic** is the one you know.
- 🕹️ A fourth layout, **Arcade**: a cabinet's select screen, with the games as big tiles in a strip
  across the TV (the one you're on lit up), P1 / P2 badges along the bottom, and "Insert coin" on a
  free seat. On phones the games become a grid of tiles.
- ☀️ A fourth theme, **Daylight**: bright paper and ink for a sunny room. The lobby, setup, Settings,
  leaderboard and trophies go light; each game keeps its own dark look while you play.

## 0.1.45

- 🎨 Pick a look for the TV and the phones in Settings (⚙ → Theme): **Default**, **Neon** (the two
  players face off across a split screen, with big scoreboard names) or **Cozy** (games as boxes on
  a shelf, warm wood and paper, players as game pieces). Every screen changes straight away, and
  each theme shows a small picture of itself to choose from.
- 🎨 Neon and Cozy also dress the match: the top bar during a game, the winner's name when it ends,
  the scoreboards, the Rematch button, the leaderboard and trophy pages, and a little entrance when
  a player sits down or a game opens.
- ⚙ Settings has no password any more, so it can't be forgotten: Player 1's phone opens it from its
  menu (⋯ → ⚙ Settings). It still only opens at home, never over the internet.
- 🎨 Every game's "You win!" and "X wins the match!" now wears the theme too (Neon's scoreboard
  letters in your colour, Cozy's serif in mustard). Kart Race keeps its own look for now.
- 🎮 Player 1 can change the theme from a controller on the TV: RT in the lobby, ← → to choose, A to pick.
- 🔊 Each theme sounds its own when a player sits down or leaves: a chalk scrape in Neon, a knock
  on the wooden table in Cozy.

## 0.1.44

- 🧱 Brick Breaker Duel: a missed ball now puts back the last brick you broke, so keeping the ball
  in play counts (and the computer's levels really differ). Prefer the old way? Pick "Missed ball:
  just a moment lost" on the setup screen.
- 🏒 Air Hockey: a new setup option, "Own goals: don't count". Then your own goal bounces out a puck
  you touched last, so a save that goes wrong doesn't score for them. Own goals still count unless
  you pick it.

## 0.1.43

- 🏷️ The hub on a Raspberry Pi or in Docker shows its real version on the TV (it always said
  v0.1.0), so you can tell which release it's running.
- 💾 A board, card or casino game in progress (Checkers, Connect Four, Mancala, Dots and Boxes,
  Battleship, Dice Party, Crazy Eights, Liar's Dice, Memory Match) survives an update or a power cut:
  the TV picks it up where it was, with the same turn and the time left on the clock, and the
  phones get their seats back.

## 0.1.42

- 🏎️ Kart Race: phones get a quarter of the data they did during a race (the TV still draws the
  whole track), so racing stays smooth on a busy Wi-Fi.
- 🏒 Air Hockey: the computer moves like a person. Its mallet speeds up, eases off and curves
  round instead of darting about, and it doesn't twitch. It plays smarter too: Normal stays in line
  with your shots instead of knocking them into its own goal, and Hard reads the puck, meets it
  and shoots at the open side of your goal, or off a rail. Each level is a real step up.
- 🏓 Pong: the computer's paddle speeds up and eases off like a person's instead of darting, and on
  Hard it angles the ball away from your paddle.
- 🛡️ Tank Duel: the computer leads its shots (it aims where you'll be), and it steers all the time
  on what it saw a moment ago, as you do, instead of in jerky steps. Hard is now an even fight for
  most players, and Normal sits between it and Easy.
- 🧱 Brick Breaker Duel: the computer's paddle speeds up and eases off like a person's instead of
  darting.
- ☄️ Asteroid Duel: the computer steers all the time on what it saw a moment ago, as you do, and
  fires once it's lined up on your ship instead of waiting to be dead on. Hard is a tough fight.

## 0.1.41

- 🗂️ A shorter lobby: the board games (Checkers, Connect Four, Mancala, Dots and Boxes, Battleship,
  Dice Party) are in a 🎲 Board Games folder, and Crazy Eights, Memory Match and Liar's Dice in
  🃏 Cards & Casino. Open one to pick a game; ◀ All games (or B on a controller) goes back.

## 0.1.40

- 🏎️ Kart Race 3D: the computer in player 2's seat drives the big tracks properly now: it holds a
  steady line, only brakes for bends it can't take, and laps in about 45 seconds on Hard (Easy and
  Normal are a little slower), instead of weaving and braking its way round.

## 0.1.39

- 🦖 Kart Race 3D: Rex drives for real now: his hands stay on a turning steering wheel, his body leans
  and rocks with the drive, his tail swishes, and he tosses items forward or drops them behind.

## 0.1.38

- 🛞 Kart Race 3D: Rex's 3D wheels look like his kart's wheels now, with a black tyre, a yellow rim and a
  red hub, and they stay clean as they spin.
- 👀 Kart Race 3D: look behind you and glance left or right while you drive: hold Look back on
  the phone, or use a controller's right stick.
- 🏁 Kart Race 3D's tracks are much bigger: a road wide enough for the whole pack and laps of about
  a minute (3D races now default to 3 laps). Lap records start fresh on the bigger tracks.

## 0.1.37

- 🏎️ Kart Race 3D has a new garage, player-by-player racing HUD, a course map, clearer item and
  drift-boost displays, and a podium with times and ready states. Phone controls show item names
  and when to release a charged drift. Loading now has feedback and retry (controller X).
- 🏜️ Kart Race 3D races on five big new tracks built for 3D, with a road twice as wide and laps
  of about a minute: Sunset Canyon Rally, Speedway Oval, Grand Prix Circuit, Serpent Pass and
  Zigzag Ridge.
- 🏁 Kart Race 3D has an 8-kart Cup: you two and six computer racers, five races on the big
  tracks, points for every place, and the leader starts at the back.
- 🦖 Kart Race 3D: Rex now races as a real 3D kart and driver. His wheels spin and steer, he
  turns the wheel, looks into bends, throws his arms up when he spins out and pumps his fist
  when he wins.
- 🏜️ Kart Race 3D: Sunset Canyon Rally now races through its canyon: red rock cliffs and mesas,
  two rock arches over the road, a dam in the distance and the festival paddock by the start.

## 0.1.36

- 🥁 Four new minigames, in Party Minigames and on the Party Board: Drum Beat (hit the notes on the
  beat), Musical Chairs (sit first when the music stops, but don't fall for a dip), Archery (steady
  the bow and allow for the wind, in 3D) and Curling (slide, curl and sweep to the button, in 3D).

## 0.1.35

- ✨ HD graphics in the 3D games: lava, lanterns, sparks and the goal ring glow, shiny things
  (bowling balls, marbles, metal) catch the light, and shadows are much sharper. The new HD
  button in the TV's top bar switches a slower TV to the standard look.
- 🤖 The computer plays smarter where its levels barely differed: in Penalty Shootout Hard learns
  your habits (where you kick, where you dive), in Bumper Cars it leads you and turns to face a
  ram, in Crazy Eights it remembers the suits you had to draw on, and as Dungeon Duo's Fighter it
  stays out of the Warden's reach while it swings.

## 0.1.34

- 🏗️ New co-op game: Tower Stack. Take turns dropping sliding blocks onto one tower in 3D:
  whatever hangs over is cut off, a dead-on drop keeps it whole, and three in a row grows it back.
  Stars for how high you get, with the computer as your teammate if you like.

## 0.1.33

- 🎳 New game: Bowling. Ten-pin in 3D with real pin action: drag the ball to where you stand,
  swipe it up the lane, and curve the swipe for a hook into the pocket (or swing the phone). Ten
  frames or a quick five, bumpers if you want them, and a computer opponent.
- 🏆 Two new trophies: a Turkey (three strikes in a row) and a Perfect game (300).

## 0.1.32

- 🏰 Dungeon Duo: two new floors before the Warden. The Ice Caves (a frozen lake you slide on, and
  big slimes that split in two) and the Armory (arrow traps on a beat, and an iron door that needs
  you both on its levers at once). Pick a short 3-floor quest in the setup if you're in a hurry.
- 🎱 Marble Maze: three new mazes with moving parts: a sliding block, conveyor belts, and a button
  that opens a gate.
- 🍔 Kitchen Rush: a fourth kitchen, the Food Truck, with a cheese crate, two new dishes (the
  cheeseburger and the deluxe) and serving hatches that take turns.

## 0.1.31

- 🌍 Online play: press Invite someone online on the TV and a friend somewhere else can join from
  their phone, with a remote screen link to watch your TV from theirs. No account or setup (a
  free Cloudflare quick tunnel); Settings can turn it off or use your own tunnel address.
- While someone's online, the game cards show which games play great that way and which are
  best in the same room.

## 0.1.30

- 🏆 Trophies: 25 achievements for each player, from your first win and a clean sweep to beating
  the Stone Warden, a perfect Trivia Quest and beating the Hard computer in every game. A card
  slides in on the TV and your phone when you earn one; see them all from the leaderboard or
  🏆 My trophies in your phone's ⋯ menu.
- 🤖 Practice: with the computer seated, each game card shows the levels you've beaten it on (or
  your best stars with it as your teammate). It still stays out of the leaderboard.

## 0.1.29

- 🤖 The computer is now a teammate in the co-op games: Kitchen Rush (it cooks, plates up and
  serves, and passes things over in the Split kitchen), Dungeon Duo (it fights, heals, revives you,
  holds the gate and finds the stairs) and Marble Maze (it tilts its half of the board). Easy,
  Normal or Hard, as in the other games.
- Every game that can have a computer player now has one: the drawing and bluffing games still
  need a person.

## 0.1.28

- 🤖 The computer now plays Party Board, Party Minigames and Micro Mayhem, every minigame, at
  Easy, Normal or Hard. It reacts and times its taps like a person, remembers paths and shapes about
  as well as one, and on the board heads for the star and uses its items.
- With the computer in the other seat, the phone-only minigames (Bowling Bash, Sword Duel, Pour
  It, Mirror Me) still come up, and the TV no longer shows a controller cursor for it.

## 0.1.27

- 🤖 The computer now plays Crazy Eights, Liar's Dice, Anagram Duel and Word Race, at Easy, Normal
  or Hard. These need just your phone against it. It only knows its own cards and dice, plays the
  odds, and takes a person's time to find words.
- Crazy Eights: a hand where nothing's left to draw and neither of you can play now ends (it goes to
  whoever holds fewer points) instead of passing back and forth for ever.

## 0.1.26

- 🤖 The computer now plays Falling Blocks Battle, Bomb Duel, Kart Race and Kart Race 3D (races and
  balloon battles) and Tower Defence Duel, at Easy, Normal or Hard. In Kart Race it readies up on
  the menus once you have.
- Kart Race: if a player's phone drops out on the setup or standings screen, the other no longer
  waits for them to press Ready (they count as ready, as was always meant).

## 0.1.25

- 🤖 The computer now plays Volley Blobs, Brick Breaker Duel, Rally Tennis, Sumo, Asteroid Duel and
  Mini Golf, at Easy, Normal or Hard.
- 🤖 The computer reacts like a person in the action games: it answers what it sees a human
  reaction time later (a quick player's on Hard, never faster), times what it sees coming with a
  person's error, and has a wobble in its aim and the odd misjudgement.
- With the computer seated, the games it's learning next say ⏳ Coming soon: Falling Blocks Battle,
  Bomb Duel, Kart Race, Tower Defence Duel, the card and word games, the party games, and a
  teammate in the co-op games.

## 0.1.24

- 🤖 The computer now plays the action games too: Pong, Air Hockey, Snake Battle, Light Cycles
  and Tank Duel, and Word Hunt (it searches the grid like you do), at Easy, Normal or Hard.
- Air Hockey: a mallet can no longer push the puck through a wall in a corner, and a puck resting
  in a corner (where no mallet can get behind it) drifts back out after a moment.

## 0.1.23

- 🤖 The computer now plays Connect Four, Checkers, Dots and Boxes, Mancala, Rock Paper Scissors,
  Memory Match, Dice Party, Quiz Battle, Reaction Duel and Trivia Quest (as your teammate), at
  Easy, Normal or Hard.
- With the computer seated, the games it can't play stay in the list, greyed out; the ones coming
  next (Pong, Air Hockey, Snake Battle, Light Cycles, Tank Duel, Word Hunt) say ⏳ Coming soon.

## 0.1.22

- 🤖 Play the computer: with a seat free, pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the TV or your phone (or LT
  on a controller). It plays Battleship, Classic and Ultimate (it picks a commander and uses its
  skills and special), only sees what its own phone would, and its games don't count in the
  leaderboard. Someone joining from the lobby takes its seat.

## 0.1.21

- Ultimate Battleship: every move now plays out on the TV and phones (explosions along the line,
  a torpedo's wake, sonar sweeps, ships going down, frost for Deep Freeze).
- Ultimate Battleship: two new commanders, 🌪️ Admiral Tempest (a cross and a U, and Typhoon) and
  🦑 Captain Kraken (a hidden cross-shaped Leviathan, and Deep Strike down a whole column).
- Ultimate Battleship: two new seas. In the **Fog Bank**, shots into the fog (and what they earn)
  show only on your next turn, and torpedoes lose their way in it. The **Volcano** erupts after
  8 turns and hits every ship square next to the lava; if it sinks both fleets, it's a draw.

## 0.1.20

- **⚡ Ultimate Battleship** (pick it in Battleship's setup): five commanders, each with their own
  fleet (some of it L, T and Z shaped) and a special, ship skills paid with energy (air strikes,
  sonar, torpedoes, shields and more), and four seas with islands, reefs and ice.

## 0.1.19

- Memory Match: a **Race** mode for two phones. The same cards on each phone, you both flip at
  once on your own board, and the first to clear every pair wins.
- Word Race: a **Race** mode. The first to 20, 35 or 50 points takes the round, and words you
  both found count for both.

## 0.1.18

- A **Timer** choice in setup for the games whose clock only keeps things moving: **Timed**
  (the usual times, shown on the button) or **No timer** (a turn or answer waits for you).
  Mind Meld and Trivia Quest now start on No timer (pick Timed for the old clock; in Trivia
  Quest with no timer every right answer hits as hard as a middling-fast one). Fake Out, Crazy
  Eights, Checkers, Mancala, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes, Draw Duel, Battleship and Memory
  Match start timed.

## 0.1.17

- **📱 No TV needed** on five action games: Pong's whole court on your phone (your paddle at
  the bottom), Brick Breaker Duel's wall and balls, Snake Battle's and Light Cycles' arena above
  the pad, and in Tower Defence Duel the creeps on your road plus a small map of your rival's.
- Draw Duel shows your rival's drawing on your phone at the reveal, and Word Race lists their
  words under yours at the end of a round.

## 0.1.16

- Word Hunt: a **Race** mode for two phones. Your own copy of the grid on your phone, your finds
  kept to yourself, and the first to find every word takes it.
- **📱 No TV needed** on 22 more games: the board, card, word and quiz games, where your phone
  shows everything. Fake Out and Liar's Dice now show their reveals on the phones too, and
  the time left for your turn or answer shows in your phone's header.

## 0.1.15

- New co-op game: **Dungeon Duo**, in 3D. A Fighter and a Healer fight down three floors
  together: keys and doors, spikes, a gate one of you holds open, skeletons, and the Stone Warden
  at the bottom. Revive each other when one of you goes down.
- The 3D games (Marble Maze, the 3D minigames and Party Board) are sharper, with soft shadows.

## 0.1.14

- New game: **Kart Race 3D**. Kart Race on a split screen, with a camera behind each kart, so you
  race down the road instead of watching from above. Same racers, tracks, items and battles.
- Going back to the lobby while a game is still loading no longer leaves the TV stuck on it.
- Dice Party: a **📱 Handheld** screen. Your phone shows the whole scorecard, with the dice and
  Roll at the bottom: play it without the TV.
- New labels on the games: **📱 No TV needed** and **🤸 Motion** (tilt or move your phone to
  play).

## 0.1.13

- New game: **Tower Defence Duel**. Build towers to stop the creeps on your road, and spend
  gold to send creeps down your rival's. Run out of lives and you lose.

## 0.1.12

- New card game: **Crazy Eights**, your hand secret on your phone. Match the suit or rank, eights
  are wild, 2s make them draw. First to empty their hand wins. Needs two phones.
- New co-op game: **Kitchen Rush**. Chop, cook, plate up and serve the orders before they run
  out, together. Three kitchens, one split down the middle so you pass things across.

## 0.1.11

- New co-op game: **Trivia Quest**. Answer quiz questions together to beat three monsters (a Goo
  Blob, a Stone Golem and the Dragon) before your shared hearts run out. Both right is a team
  strike; each of you has one 50/50. Up to 9 ⭐.
- New game: **Fake Out**. Write a fake answer to a strange-but-true fact, then spot the real
  one among the fakes. Find the truth for 1000, fool your rival for 500. Needs two phones.

## 0.1.10

- Party Board: two new maps. **🌉 Sky Bridges**: a drawbridge across the middle goes up and
  down every two rounds (up, you go the long way round). **🛒 Market Square**: two shops, and
  prices change every round, from half price to double; the star's price moves too.
- Kart Race: **🎈 Battle** mode. Three balloons each in a wide arena (Donut, Peanut or Plaza);
  every spin-out pops one. Pop all of theirs, or have more left when the 2 minutes are up.

## 0.1.9

- New game: **Volley Blobs**: beach volleyball for two bouncy blobs. Slide, jump and bounce the
  ball over the net; up to 3 touches a side.
- New game: **Brick Breaker Duel**: race to break your wall first, side by side. Power bricks
  shrink your rival's paddle, give you an extra ball or add a row to their wall.
- New game: **Asteroid Duel**: two ships, drifting rocks and edges that wrap round. Turn, thrust
  and shoot; three lives a round.
- New game: **Rally Tennis**: tennis from above. Your player runs to the ball; you time the swing
  and aim. Spot-on timing hits hardest; aim wide and mistime it, and it's out.
- New game: **Falling Blocks Battle**: side-by-side falling blocks with the same pieces for both.
  Clear lines to send junk rows to your rival; fill your well and you lose the round.

## 0.1.8

- **What's new**: after an update, the TV shows what changed. Tap the version number (bottom
  right) to see it again.
- New Party Minigames: **Hurdle Dash** (jump the hurdles in time), **Laser Dodge** (jump the low
  beams, duck the high ones) and **Pop the Most** (pop the balloons in your colour).
- New Party Minigames: **Safe Cracker** (turn the dial until it clicks), **Reflex Grid** (tap the
  lights before they fade) and **Mirror Me** (remember a shape and draw it from memory).
- New Party Minigames: **Cannon Lob** (lob shots over the wall at your rival's tower, with the
  wind) and **Stack Attack** (catch the falling crates and keep your stack from toppling).
- New 3D Party Minigames: **Bumper Cars** (ram your rival in the side or from behind; head-on
  doesn't count) and **Paint Grab** (paint the most floor tiles your colour).

## 0.1.7

- New game: **Anagram Duel**: the same five letters for both of you; the first to make a word
  takes the round.
- New game: **Word Hunt**: a word search race; each word goes to whoever marks it first.

- Started from Steam (Game Mode on the Deck), the app no longer opens to a black screen.
