# What's new

What changed in each desktop app release, in plain words. The app shows the new lines on the TV
after it updates (the What's new card), and each GitHub release uses its version's lines as its
notes. Newest first; one `## x.y.z` heading per release and one `- ` line per change.

## 0.1.10

- Party Board: two new maps. **🌉 Sky Bridges**: a drawbridge across the middle goes up and
  down every two rounds (up, you go the long way round). **🛒 Market Square**: two shops, and
  prices change every round, from half price to double; the star's price moves too.
- Kart Race: **🎈 Battle** mode. Three balloons each in a wide arena (Donut, Peanut or Plaza);
  every spin-out pops one. Pop all of theirs, or have more left when the 2 minutes are up.

## 0.1.9

- New game: **Volley Blobs**: beach volleyball for two bouncy blobs. Slide, jump and bounce the
  ball over the net; up to 3 touches a side.
- New game: **Brick Breaker Duel**: race to break your wall first, side by side. Power bricks
  shrink your rival's paddle, give you an extra ball or add a row to their wall.
- New game: **Asteroid Duel**: two ships, drifting rocks and edges that wrap round. Turn, thrust
  and shoot; three lives a round.
- New game: **Rally Tennis**: tennis from above. Your player runs to the ball; you time the swing
  and aim. Spot-on timing hits hardest; aim wide and mistime it, and it's out.
- New game: **Falling Blocks Battle**: side-by-side falling blocks with the same pieces for both.
  Clear lines to send junk rows to your rival; fill your well and you lose the round.

## 0.1.8

- **What's new**: after an update, the TV shows what changed. Tap the version number (bottom
  right) to see it again.
- New Party Minigames: **Hurdle Dash** (jump the hurdles in time), **Laser Dodge** (jump the low
  beams, duck the high ones) and **Pop the Most** (pop the balloons in your colour).
- New Party Minigames: **Safe Cracker** (turn the dial until it clicks), **Reflex Grid** (tap the
  lights before they fade) and **Mirror Me** (remember a shape and draw it from memory).
- New Party Minigames: **Cannon Lob** (lob shots over the wall at your rival's tower, with the
  wind) and **Stack Attack** (catch the falling crates and keep your stack from toppling).
- New 3D Party Minigames: **Bumper Cars** (ram your rival in the side or from behind; head-on
  doesn't count) and **Paint Grab** (paint the most floor tiles your colour).

## 0.1.7

- New game: **Anagram Duel**: the same five letters for both of you; the first to make a word
  takes the round.
- New game: **Word Hunt**: a word search race; each word goes to whoever marks it first.
