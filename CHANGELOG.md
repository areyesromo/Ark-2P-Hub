# What's new

What changed in each desktop app release, in plain words. The app shows the new lines on the TV
after it updates (the What's new card), and each GitHub release uses its version's lines as its
notes. Newest first; one `## x.y.z` heading per release and one `- ` line per change.

## 0.1.75

- 🎰 Video Poker: you both get the same five cards and the same new ones; hold the best and draw,
  and the better hand takes the pot. Play chips only.
- 🐟 Go Fish: ask for a rank you hold, or go fish; four of a kind is a book, and the most books wins.
- ♠️ Spades for two: draw your hand a card at a time, bid your tricks, then play with spades as
  trumps. Make your bid and mind the bags. First to 300.
- 📦 Which Box: one hides the prize in one of three boxes, the other guesses which; then you swap.
- ✋ Odd or Even: both show one to five fingers in secret; an odd total is Odd's point, even is Even's.
- 🤖 All five can be played against the computer, at three levels.

## 0.1.74

- ♠️ Heads-up Hold'em: Texas hold'em for two with play chips, your two cards secret on your phone,
  the blinds going up as you play. The computer plays at three levels.
- ⚡ Speed: both at once, slap cards one higher or one lower onto the middle piles; first to play
  every card takes the round.
- 📍 Cribbage: lay two in the crib, peg fifteens, pairs and runs to 31, count your hand on a peg
  board. First to 121 (or 61).
- 🍀 Lucky Seven: our own push-your-luck card game: flip or bank, a repeat busts you, seven
  different numbers is a Lucky Seven. First to 200.
- 🃏 A new party minigame, Higher or Lower: call the next card in secret; right calls in a row
  score more.

## 0.1.73

- 🚰 Pipes, a new 1 player puzzle: turn the pipe pieces until the water from the tap reaches every
  one, with no open ends. 5 × 5 to 9 × 9, and a wrap-round mode.
- ⋖ Futoshiki, a new 1 player puzzle: each number once in every row and column, keeping the < and >
  signs between squares. 4 × 4 to 7 × 7, with pencil marks.
- 📍 Peg Solitaire, a new 1 player puzzle: jump the pegs until one is left (in the middle for a
  perfect game), on the English or European board or a warm-up.
- 🌉 Bridges, a new 1 player puzzle (Hashi): join the islands with bridges, as many as each number.
  30 levels and random boards.
- ➰ Slitherlink, a new 1 player puzzle: draw one loop round the dots to match the numbers.
  30 levels and random boards.

## 0.1.72

- ☀️ Tango, a new 1 player puzzle: fill the grid with suns and moons (no three alike in a line, as
  many of each in every row and column, and keep the = and × signs). 30 levels and random boards.
- 〰️ Zip, a new 1 player puzzle: draw one path through the numbers in order that fills every
  square. 30 levels (walls in the later ones) and random boards.
- 🚗 Traffic Jam, a new 1 player puzzle: slide the cars and trucks so the red car can drive out.
  40 levels, each with a par, and a pack of random car parks.

## 0.1.60

- 🎮 Five new games, each against a friend or the computer:
  - ⚫ Reversi: trap a line of your rival's discs between two of yours and they all flip to your
    colour. Most discs wins.
  - 💣 Mine Duel: one minefield, and you want the mines. Dig one up and go again; dig a safe
    square and it's their turn. First to 13 of the 25.
  - 🃏 Blackjack: beat the dealer for play chips. You both get the same cards, so the better
    player wins, not the luckier one.
  - 🟩 Five Letters: the same hidden word for you both, six guesses each, coloured clues. Fewest
    guesses wins the round. You see their colours, never their letters.
  - 🔢 Make 24: four numbers, join them with + − × ÷ until one is left. First to make 24.

## 0.1.59

  your chest is (🔥 hot, ♨️ warm, 🌤️ cool, ❄️ cold). First to dig up three chests wins. In Party
  Minigames and Party Board, against a friend or the computer (which works it out like a person).

## 0.1.57

- ⚡ Seven new quick games in Micro Mayhem:

  - 🔢 Bigger number: pick the bigger of two numbers, three times running.
  - 🚦 Lights out: five lights go out one by one. Tap as the last one goes, not before.
  - 🃏 Snap: cards turn over on the TV. First to snap on a matching pair wins; a wrong snap loses.
  - 🛞 Pump it: pump the tyre up into the green, but not past it (it bursts).
  - 🎁 Catch it: let go of the present when the claw is over your basket.
  - 🔴 Dodge ball: swipe up or down out of the way of the balls.
  The computer plays them all, like a person.

## 0.1.56

- 🤖 The computer no longer loses to someone who does nothing:
  - Pong: after misjudging one ball, it missed every serve that came straight at it; fixed.
  - Air Hockey: it shoots for the open side of your goal, and doesn't chase a fast rebound into its own.
  - Bomb Duel: it no longer freezes on its own bomb, and it heads for the middle before the walls close in.
  - Tank Duel: it fires at a tank that stands still.
  - Snake (Easy) turns away from a wall in time, and Light Cycles (Easy) swerves out of a head-on.
  - Mancala (Easy) looks a move further ahead, and Memory Match (Easy) remembers more.
  - Mini Golf: it no longer stands still for good once every ball has stopped (it was still

  - Connect Four (Easy) blocks a four you're about to make, and Dots & Boxes (Easy) takes a free box.
- 📈 Tap the version number at the bottom right during a game to show the frame rate (to see how

- 🏒 Air Hockey: a slow puck left on one side for 7 seconds drifts over to the other, so nobody can stall.
- 💣 Bomb Duel: a turn pressed a moment after a junction still takes (a phone's slight delay

- 🔢 A 1 or 2 on the pieces that share the screen (mallets, sumo discs, tanks, ships, bombers, snakes,

- 🏒 Air Hockey: a new setup option, "Own goals: don't count". Then your own goal bounces out a puck

## 0.1.42

- 🏎️ Kart Race: phones get a quarter of the data they did during a race (the TV still draws the
  whole track), so racing stays smooth on a busy Wi-Fi.
- 🏒 Air Hockey: the computer moves like a person. Its mallet speeds up, eases off and curves

  most players, and Normal sits between it and Easy.
- 🧱 Brick Breaker Duel: the computer's paddle speeds up and eases off like a person's instead of
  darting.
- ☄️ Asteroid Duel: the computer steers all the time on what it saw a moment ago, as you do, and
